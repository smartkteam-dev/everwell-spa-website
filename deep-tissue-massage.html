What is deep tissue massage?

Focused care for
persistent tightness.

Deep tissue massage uses slow strokes and focused pressure to work with areas that feel tight or overworked. “Deep” does not mean pressure must be painful: your deep tissue massage therapist can adjust the pace and intensity throughout the session.

Unlike Swedish massage, which usually emphasizes broad full-body relaxation, deep tissue work commonly spends more time on selected areas. Both approaches can be relaxing; the best choice depends on your pressure preference and goals for the visit. Deep tissue massage clients often request focused attention for back and shoulder tension or tired legs.
