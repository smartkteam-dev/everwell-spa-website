Manual lymphatic drainage, sometimes called MLD massage, uses light, methodical movements rather than deep pressure. Your session at our Redmond spa is shaped around your comfort and the service options currently available.

Lymphatic massage FAQ

Before your

What is lymphatic drainage massage?

It is a gentle style of bodywork that uses light, rhythmic movements. It is different from deep tissue massage and should not feel forceful.

Where do you offer lymphatic massage?

Everwell Spa offers lymphatic bodywork at 7861 Gilman St in Redmond, also convenient for many clients throughout Redmond and the Eastside.

Is this medical treatment?

No. Our massage service does not diagnose, treat or guarantee improvement of a medical condition. Ask your healthcare professional whether massage is suitable for you.
