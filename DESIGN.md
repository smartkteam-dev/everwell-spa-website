# Everwell Spa Design System

## Direction
Everwell is a quiet, editorial spa experience for visitors looking for restorative massage care in the Eastside. The visual world preserves the site's purple identity with deep plum, lavender paper, mauve details, and intimate treatment photography.

## Type
- Display: Playfair Display, with italic emphasis for the body's softer voice.
- Body: DM Sans for clear service details, navigation, and calls to action.
- Labels: uppercase DM Sans with generous tracking.

## Color
- Ink: `#40304b`
- Deep plum: `#2d2038`
- Warm paper: `#fcf9fd`
- Lavender paper: `#f1eaf3`
- Mauve: `#a56f9d`
- Muted text: `#756979`

## Components
- Full-bleed hero with a direct booking action.
- Image-led treatment panels with bottom-aligned readable overlays.
- Editorial split sections with numbered proof points.
- Utility navigation collapses to an accessible disclosure menu below 800px.
- Booking links use the Square appointment destination supplied by the business.

## Motion
Content reveals upward once as it enters the viewport. Treatment photography lifts subtly on hover. Reduced-motion users receive the complete static layout without transitions.

## Responsive rules
Desktop uses wide editorial columns and a split location section. Mobile becomes a single reading column, preserves the large type hierarchy, and keeps booking actions thumb-friendly.
