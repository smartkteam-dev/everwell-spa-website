What is Swedish massage?

A slower pace
for body and mind.

Swedish massage uses long, flowing strokes, gentle kneading and comfortable pressure across the body. Compared with more focused techniques, the rhythm is typically broad and soothing. It is designed as a relaxation and wellness service, not as medical treatment.

People often choose Swedish massage when they feel mentally busy, physically tired or simply want restorative personal time. It can be especially inviting for first-time massage clients and anyone who prefers lighter to moderate pressure. For Redmond Swedish massage clients, the experience offers a quiet pause at our local spa.
